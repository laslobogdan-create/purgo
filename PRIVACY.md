# Privacy policy

This program will not transfer any information to other networked systems unless specifically requested by the user or the person installing or operating it.

## In detail

- Purgo runs entirely on your computer. It has **no telemetry, no analytics, no crash reporting, no accounts and no automatic update checks**.
- The list of installed programs, the leftover scans and everything else Purgo shows are read locally and never leave your PC.
- Purgo only stores two things locally: your UI preferences (in the app's own data folder) and registry backups of the keys it removes, in `C:\ProgramData\Purgo\Backups`.
- The only network-related actions are the **"Search online"** and **"Open website"** buttons. They open your default web browser on a search engine or the program's own website, and only when you click them.
